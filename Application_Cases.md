# Application Cases — FAWOO NANOTECH (적용 사례)

_Last updated: 2026-11-12. Company tests. Each case gives the date, setup and what was observed.
Customer names are withheld._

## 1. Alkaline solution neutralization with CO₂ nanobubbles (강알칼리 중화)

- **Setup:** Ca(OH)₂ (calcium hydroxide) solution, start pH 12.50 at 18.3 ℃; CO₂ fed through the
  nanobubble generator at 4 bar.
- **Result:**

| CO₂ flow (LPM) | 3.3 | 7.2 | 10 | 22 | 30 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Final pH | 7.96 | 6.54 | 5.93 | 5.55 | 5.05 |

- **Why it matters:** strongly alkaline wastewater (for example from naphtha cracking or parts
  washing) is usually neutralized with sulfuric or hydrochloric acid. CO₂ does the same job and
  uses a gas that would otherwise be emitted.
- Source: technical brochure (2026-01); Research Square preprint on CO₂ dissolution.

## 2. Mine-water metal removal with ozone nanobubble water (갱내수 중금속 산화)

- **Setup:** acidic mine water from a domestic site mixed with ozone nanobubble water at
  increasing ratios.

| Mine water : NB ozone water | 1 : 0 | 1 : ¼ | 1 : ½ | 1 : 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| pH | 2.56 | 2.66 | 2.77 | 3.03 |
| Fe (mg/L) | 20 | 8.38 | 2.07 | 0.92 |
| Mn (mg/L) | 3.1 | 2.38 | 1.05 | 0.4 |

- **Observation:** ozone oxidizes dissolved Fe²⁺ and Mn²⁺ to insoluble hydroxides and oxides that
  settle out. The company reports that from a 1 : 1 ratio the metals stayed within discharge limits.
- Source: technical brochure (2026-01).

## 3. Waste-oil water separation (폐유 유수 분리)

- **Date / place:** 2024-05-02, Bucheon test site.
- **Setup:** 70 L of waste oil received from a customer, circulated through a nanobubble
  generator for 2.5 h with O₂ at 1.5 LPM.
- **Observation:** a separated water layer formed; water was more than 50% of the circulated volume
  and could be drawn off for reuse. No emulsifier was used.
- Source: technical brochure (2026-01).

## 4. Pipe scale and rust removal (배관 스케일 제거)

- **Setup:** corroded sections of an old heat-exchange radiator (32A S-pipe, 25A L-pipe);
  nanobubble water circulated 24 h a day with a 350 W pump (26 L/min) through a 25 mm × 5 m
  nano-tube.
- **Observation:** sludge discharged each week and the pipe interior was photographed weekly for
  three to four weeks. Results are shown as photographs only; no removal percentage is claimed.
- Source: technical brochure (2026-01); pamphlet FN2506-E03.

## 5. Oil-contaminated soil (기름 오염토)

- **Date / place:** 2024-06-22, Bucheon lab.
- **Setup:** 15 g of oil-contaminated soil in 400 mL of O₂ nanobubble water, then repeated washes
  with ozone nanobubble water (3.8 ppm), 3 h settling each time.
- **Observation:** oil separated and floated; after repeated washes the soil was visually clean.
- Source: technical brochure (2026-01).

## 6. Oxygen and pH in water (산소수·pH)

- Modular generator with O₂: DO 9 → 32 ppm.
- Modular generator with a small CO₂ dose: pH 8.0 → 5.82.
- Source: CES 2026 press release.
